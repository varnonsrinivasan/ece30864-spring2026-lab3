(a) Agile development allows teams to adapt quickly to changing requirements compared to non-Agile approaches.
Agile delivers working software more frequently, enabling faster feedback than non-Agile methods.
Agile can result in less predictable schedules and costs than non-Agile development.
Agile requires more constant communication and stakeholder involvement than non-Agile approaches.



(b) The Scrum Master ensures the Scrum process is followed, removes obstacles, and helps the team work efficiently.
The Product Owner defines and prioritizes requirements and represents stakeholder and customer needs.

(c) 
Sprint Planning: The team discusses what work will be completed during the sprint and breaks down backlog items into tasks,
while also clarifying goals and priorities.
Daily Stand-Up: Team members share what they worked on, what they plan to work on next, and discuss any blockers or issues.
Sprint Review (Retrospective): The team reviews completed work with stakeholders and discusses what went well, what didn’t, 
and how to improve in the next sprint.
